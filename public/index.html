Pulse Watch

Fitbit heart rate visualizer and .csv exporter, 2018 to 2026

This app has been retired

Pulse Watch was built on the Fitbit Web API, which Google shut down on November 2, 2026. The app can no longer connect to Fitbit accounts.

Thank you to everyone who used it to download their heart rate data. You can still get a copy of your own data through Google Takeout.
